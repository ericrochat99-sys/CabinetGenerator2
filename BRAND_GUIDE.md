# ForgeCase Brand Guide v1.0

## Brand position

ForgeCase is a premium Division 12 commercial casework manufacturer focused on durable, architect-driven cabinetry for education, healthcare, government, laboratory, and commercial facilities.

**Personality:** Industrial, modern, precise, dependable, engineered, and professional.

**Brand promise:** Built for the environments that matter most.

## Mission

To manufacture commercial casework that combines engineering precision, exceptional durability, and modern design while making specification, ordering, and installation easier for contractors and architects.

## Visual identity

- **Forge Black:** `#2D3036` — logo, text, cabinetry outlines, drawings, and primary structure.
- **Forge Bronze:** `#A77747` — restrained accents, highlights, buttons, and headings.
- **White:** `#FFFFFF` — clean architectural background.
- **Light Gray:** `#E9ECEF` — drawings, tables, controls, and secondary backgrounds.
- Primary typography should be a wide geometric sans serif such as Gotham, Eurostile Next, Nexa Bold, Avenir Next, or Montserrat SemiBold.
- Body and specification typography should use Inter or Source Sans Pro.

## Logo usage

The angular mark combines an abstract F, stacked cabinet panels, layered materials, engineered construction, strength, and precision. Maintain generous clear space and original proportions. Approved treatments are the dark-and-bronze mark on white, all black, white on dark, or single-color engraving.

Never rotate, distort, stretch, add gradients, bevels, shadows, or substitute arbitrary accent colors.

## Graphic language

Use thin rules, grids, white space, square corners, panel layouts, cabinet elevations, and technical drawing cues. Avoid swooshes, curves, glossy effects, flames, and excessive wood textures.

## Photography

Favor manufacturing, CNC equipment, edge banding, engineered wood, clean fabrication, commercial interiors, education, laboratories, healthcare, and architectural details. Avoid staged residential kitchens.

## Voice

Confident, technical, direct, and dependable. Reinforce precision, strength, engineering, commercial durability, architecture, modern manufacturing, reliability, and professionalism. Avoid exaggerated terms such as “revolutionary” and “disruptive.”
